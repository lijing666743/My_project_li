# Claim-Strength Phrase Policy

## Purpose

Phrase refinement can make wording clearer, denser, and more journal-like, but it must not upgrade evidence.

## Preferred Claim Verbs

Use measured verbs when evidence is bounded:

- formulate
- investigate
- develop
- design
- derive
- decompose
- approximate
- evaluate
- show
- indicate
- suggest
- illustrate
- validate within the tested setting

Use stronger verbs only when admitted by the gate:

- demonstrate
- prove
- guarantee
- outperform
- robustly improve
- generalize
- achieve optimality

## High-Risk Phrases

Flag or downgrade unless evidence supports them:

- robust
- optimal
- globally optimal
- real-world
- practical deployment
- scalable
- generalizable
- superior
- state-of-the-art
- significantly better
- proves

## Benchmark Language

When comparing methods, preserve:

- benchmark identity
- scenario boundary
- metric and unit
- seed or confidence boundary if applicable
- whether the comparison is simulation-only, diagnostic, or manuscript-grade

Do not turn a limited benchmark comparison into a general superiority claim.
