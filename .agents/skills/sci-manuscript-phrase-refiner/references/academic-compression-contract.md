# Academic Compression Contract

Use concise scholarly wording without deleting scientific content.

## Compress First

Remove or rewrite:
- generic openings such as "with the rapid development of"
- repeated background already established
- empty intensifiers and promotional adjectives
- duplicated contribution language
- procedural wording about how the work was produced
- meta-sentences that only announce the paper structure
- vague praise of the proposed method

## Preserve

Never remove:
- problem setting
- method identity
- comparator
- dataset or scenario boundary
- metric, unit, and numerical result
- uncertainty or limitation
- citation
- equation or symbol definition
- figure/table reference

## Rewrite Style

Prefer:
- direct subject-verb-object structure
- precise technical nouns
- short but information-dense sentences
- explicit boundary conditions
- measured claim strength
- transitions that express scientific logic rather than process chronology

Avoid:
- hype
- inflated novelty language
- vague "effective", "excellent", or "superior" without evidence
- long chains of stacked adjectives
- engineering chronology in manuscript prose
