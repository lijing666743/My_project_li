# Nature Phrase Adaptation Boundary

Use this reference only for local phrase cleanup after `sci-nature-manuscript-writer` has locked Nature-family structure, evidence allocation, and claim strength.

## Allowed

- Improve sentence flow, concision, and cross-disciplinary readability.
- Remove engineering residue and over-technical implementation wording.
- Downgrade overclaiming verbs when the evidence gate requires restraint.
- Preserve Nature-style figure-led transitions already established by the Nature writer.

## Not Allowed

- Do not restructure the Nature main text or SI; route that back to `sci-nature-manuscript-writer`.
- Do not copy Academic Phrasebank or external `nature-skills` wording.
- Do not turn style patterns into evidence.
- Do not add novelty, mechanism, broad impact, optimality, robustness, or generalization claims.
