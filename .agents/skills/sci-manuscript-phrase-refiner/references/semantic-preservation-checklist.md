# Semantic Preservation Checklist

Before returning rewritten prose, verify that the rewrite preserves:
- central claim
- claim strength
- comparator and baseline scope
- method component identity
- variables, equations, symbols, and definitions
- numerical values, units, and statistical qualifiers
- scenario, dataset, and experimental boundary
- citation anchors
- limitation and uncertainty
- figure/table references
- paragraph function within the manuscript section

Allowed changes:
- remove redundancy
- reorder for logic
- downgrade unsupported claim strength when gate output requires it
- replace engineering residue with manuscript-safe terms
- split overloaded sentences
- merge repetitive sentences

Forbidden changes:
- add a new result
- add a new cause-effect relation
- remove a limitation
- broaden a scenario
- imply robustness, superiority, deployment readiness, or generalization without evidence
- remove a citation that supports a factual claim
