# Citation Candidate Export Boundary

This reference adapts the useful idea of citation metadata candidates from an external Nature-oriented citation skill, without importing its Crossref script or export code.

## Candidate Metadata Is Not Evidence

- Crossref, PubMed, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Zotero, DOI pages, and publisher pages may identify candidate sources.
- A candidate record does not verify that the paper supports a manuscript claim.
- Before using a source as evidence, inspect the paper, abstract, full text, supplied PDF, or authoritative record relevant to the claim.

## Export Handoff

- Only write or modify bibliography/export files when the user explicitly asks.
- Prefer Zotero or existing project bibliography workflows when available.
- Mark uncertain metadata, missing DOI, title mismatch, duplicate records, and venue ambiguity.
- Keep generated citation records separate from accepted manuscript bibliography until verified.

## Blockers

- Do not invent DOI, authors, pages, issue, venue, or publication year.
- Do not use metadata search to bypass literature-review inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- Do not treat a high citation count, search ranking, or source popularity as claim evidence.
