# Search And Citation Rules

## Source Priority

Use the strongest available source:

- Publisher or journal page
- DOI resolver or Crossref record
- PubMed record for biomedical papers
- arXiv record for preprints
- Semantic Scholar as discovery support, not sole proof when stronger records exist
- User-supplied PDF or bibliography metadata

## Minimum Citation Record

Do not use a source in manuscript prose unless at least these fields are known:

- Title
- Authors
- Year
- Venue or source type
- DOI, PMID, arXiv ID, stable URL, or user-supplied file reference

## Review Synthesis

Organize by role and theme:

- Foundational theory
- Direct method family
- Dataset or task precedent
- Baseline or competing approach
- Known limitation
- Reviewer's likely objection

## Claim Gate Alignment

Before saying the target manuscript is novel, state exactly what was checked:

- Competing methods
- Similar datasets or tasks
- Evaluation metrics
- Time horizon and denominator
- Known limitations or negative evidence
